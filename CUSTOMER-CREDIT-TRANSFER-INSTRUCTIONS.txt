WAYNE LOGS — CUSTOMER CREDIT TRANSFERS

WHAT WAS ADDED
1. Every registered account receives one permanent WAYNE ID, such as WL-12AB34CD56.
2. The ID appears beside the customer's wallet balance and has a Copy button.
3. Customers can enter another customer's WAYNE ID, confirm the masked name, enter an amount and send credit.
4. The sender is debited and recipient is credited together in one MongoDB transaction.
5. Both customers receive a wallet transaction record and notification.

SECURITY RULES
- A customer cannot transfer credit to their own WAYNE ID.
- Transfers are rejected when the balance is insufficient.
- Each request has a unique server reference and cannot be charged twice.
- Recipient identity is checked on the server before the transfer.
- Minimum transfer: N100. Maximum transfer: N1,000,000.
- Completed transfers are final and cannot be reversed automatically.

DEPLOYMENT
Replace the whole project with this update, then push it to GitHub.
Both Vercel and Render must finish deploying because frontend and backend files changed.
No new Render environment key is required.

TESTING
1. Sign in as Customer A and copy the WAYNE ID from the Wallet page.
2. Sign in as Customer B, open Wallet, paste Customer A's ID and enter at least N100.
3. Confirm the displayed recipient name, approve the final question, and send.
4. Check both wallets and both transaction histories.

Existing customers receive their WAYNE ID automatically the next time they sign in or open Wallet.
