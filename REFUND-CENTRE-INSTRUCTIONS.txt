WAYNE LOGS — PROTECTED REFUND CENTRE

Eligible orders:
- Payment method is WAYNE LOGS wallet
- Payment status is paid
- Order has not already been refunded

Safety protections:
- Current admin password is required
- A refund reason is required
- Five incorrect passwords trigger a 15-minute refund lock
- The database credits the wallet and marks the order refunded together
- A second refund for the same order is blocked
- The customer receives a notification
- The admin audit log permanently records the refund

Important: Delivered digital inventory is not automatically returned to stock.
No new Render environment keys are required.
