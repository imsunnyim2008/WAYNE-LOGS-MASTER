WAYNE LOGS - SUCCESSFUL WALLET PURCHASE AND LOG DELIVERY

This update makes inventory-managed products complete automatically after wallet payment.

- The wallet balance is deducted atomically.
- Exactly one unused log is assigned to the order.
- The order and payment display Successful.
- The purchased login details appear immediately in My Orders.
- Customers can copy their login details with one button.
- The same inventory log cannot be assigned to another customer.
- Older paid inventory orders that were stuck as Processing are safely repaired when their owner opens My Orders.

IMPORTANT
The product must have unused items in Admin > Inventory. Each inventory line must contain one complete customer delivery record.

DEPLOY IN 3 STEPS
1. Replace all project files from this update. Keep your private .env.
2. Run git add -A, git commit -m "Complete wallet orders and deliver unique logs", then git push.
3. Wait for Render and Vercel, then test with a customer wallet purchase and open My Orders.
